Tournament System Specification

Build a professional Tournament System that supports both 1v1 and 2v2 Team tournaments.

⸻

Tournament Creation (Admin Panel)

When creating a tournament, Admin must select:

* Tournament Type
    * 1 vs 1
    * 2 vs 2 Team

⸻

1 vs 1 Tournament

Works as a normal tournament.

* One player joins.
* System manages all matches automatically.
* Player progresses individually from Group Stage to Final.

⸻

2 vs 2 Team Tournament

Team Creation

After a player joins the tournament:

* The player is initially marked as Waiting for Partner.
* The player can create a team by:
    * Searching partner by Username
    * Searching partner by Player ID
* Send Team Invitation.
* Partner can Accept or Reject.

After acceptance:

* Both players become one permanent team.
* Team Name can be:
    * Auto Generated (Example: RTR Team)
    * OR Admin/User can rename if enabled.

Example:

RTR Team

* Player A
* Player B

The team remains together throughout the tournament.

From Group Stage until Final, the team always plays together.

The Final Winner should display:

🏆 Champion
RTR Team

instead of individual player names.

⸻

Tournament Structure

Tournament supports:

* Group Stage
* Round of 32 (optional)
* Round of 16
* Quarter Final
* Semi Final
* Final

Admin can enable or disable stages depending on tournament size.

⸻

Group Stage Configuration

Admin should configure:

* Number of Group Matches
    * 3
    * 4
    * 5
    * 6
    * or any custom value

Every team/player in the same group plays exactly this number of matches.

⸻

Group Stage Schedule

Admin sets the schedule for every Group Match Round.

Example:

Group Match 1
Date: August 2
Time: 8:00 PM

→ Every group plays their first match at this schedule.

Group Match 2
Date: August 3
Time: 8:00 PM

Group Match 3
Date: August 4
Time: 8:00 PM

Continue until all configured Group Matches are completed.

The system automatically assigns opponents within each group.

⸻

Knockout Stage Schedule

After Group Stage ends, qualified players/teams advance automatically.

Admin schedules every Knockout Match individually.

Example:

Round of 32

Match 1
Date: August 4
Time: 7:00 PM

Match 2
Date: August 4
Time: 7:15 PM

Match 3
Date: August 4
Time: 7:30 PM

Match 4
Date: August 4
Time: 7:45 PM

…

Continue until all matches are completed.

This same scheduling system should work for:

* Round of 32
* Round of 16
* Quarter Final
* Semi Final
* Final

Each match should have its own:

* Date
* Time

configured by Admin.

⸻

Automatic Progression

The system should automatically:

* Generate Groups
* Generate Fixtures
* Track Match Results
* Update Standings
* Calculate Qualification
* Generate Knockout Bracket
* Advance Winners
* Declare Champion

No manual intervention should be required after scheduling.

⸻

Team Rules (2v2)

* Team cannot change partner after tournament starts.
* If one teammate leaves the tournament, the team is eliminated unless Admin replaces the player manually.
* Team statistics should be tracked together.
* Wins, Losses, Points and Qualification belong to the Team, not individual players.

⸻

Tournament Winner

At the end of the tournament display:

🏆 Champion

RTR Team

Players:

* Player A
* Player B

Also save Champion Team in tournament history.

⸻

Goal

The system should be fully automated, scalable, and suitable for professional esports tournaments with flexible scheduling, team management, group stages, and knockout brackets.
